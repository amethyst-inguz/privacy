# Privacy Policy for Wyrm

**Last updated: 7 August 2026**

Wyrm is a reading tracker. It is built to keep your library on your own device.

## The short version

Wyrm has no accounts, no servers of ours, and no analytics. Your books, notes, ratings,
reading sessions, collections and settings are stored **only on your device**. We cannot
see them, because they are never sent to us.

## What Wyrm stores on your device

- Books you add: title, author, cover image address, ISBN, page count, genre, mood tags
- Your reading progress, start and finish dates, star ratings and private notes
- Reading sessions (pages read and when)
- Collections you create
- Your settings: yearly goal, theme, reminder time

This data lives in a local database on your device. Uninstalling Wyrm deletes it. You can
export all of it to a CSV file at any time from Settings, and you can delete any book or
collection from within the app.

## Data we collect

**None.** Wyrm has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no
tracking pixels, and no user accounts. We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal
information, because none of it reaches us.

## Connections Wyrm makes to other services

Wyrm works fully offline. It contacts outside services only in these cases:

### 1. Open Library (book search and covers)

When you search for a book to add, Wyrm sends **your search text** to Open Library
(operated by the Internet Archive) to retrieve titles, authors, page counts, ISBNs and
cover images. Your library, notes and reading history are never sent.

Open Library's privacy policy: https://archive.org/about/terms.php

### 2. Bookstore links (affiliate links)

Book pages may include a link to buy a copy from Bookshop.org. This is an **affiliate
link**: if you buy something after following it, Wyrm may earn a small commission at no
extra cost to you. This is disclosed in the app next to the link.

Tapping the link opens your browser or the retailer's app. From that point the retailer's
own privacy policy applies, and they may set cookies to attribute the purchase. Wyrm does
not receive information about what you browse or buy — only aggregate commission totals
from the affiliate program.

- Bookshop.org privacy policy: https://bookshop.org/info/privacy-policy

## Permissions

- **Internet** — book search, cover images, and opening bookstore links.
- **Notifications** — only if you turn on the daily reading reminder. The reminder is
  generated on your device; nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn it off at any time in
  Settings, or in your device's notification settings.

## Children

Wyrm is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from
anyone. Since we collect no data at all, there is nothing for us to delete on request —
though you can always clear the app's data or uninstall it.

## Your rights

Because your data never leaves your device, you already have full control of it. To
access it, use Export in Settings. To delete it, delete the items in the app, clear the
app's storage in your device settings, or uninstall Wyrm.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change. Material changes will
be noted in the app's release notes.
